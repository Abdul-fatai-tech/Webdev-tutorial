Classic Garlic Butter Pasta

A comforting, simple meal that relies on high-quality olive oil and fresh garlic. Perfect for busy weeknights when you want something gourmet in under 15 minutes.

Prep: 5 mins Cook: 10 mins Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 8 oz Spaghetti or Linguine
  • 4 cloves Garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup Extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp Unsalted butter
  • Garnish options:
    • Freshly grated Parmesan cheese
    • Red pepper flakes
    • Fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.

    Make sure the water is "salty like the sea."

  2. Cook the pasta according to package instructions until al dente.
  3. While pasta cooks, heat oil and butter in a skillet over medium-low heat.
  4. Add sliced garlic and sauté until golden:
    1. Watch closely so it doesn't burn!
    2. Remove from heat once fragrant.
  5. Toss the drained pasta into the skillet with a splash of pasta water.
Nutritional Info: Approx. 450 kcal per serving.
