Jollof Rice Bread Indomie Amala Yam and Egg
Home> Welcome To My Recipe Website

Description

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, peppers, onions, and delicious spices. It is commonly served with chicken, beef, fish, or vegetables.

Preparation Time: 20 minutes
Cooking Time: 45 minutes
Servings: 4 People

↑

Description

  • 2 cups of long-grain rice
  • 4 fresh tomatoes
  • 2 red bell peppers
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paster
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • salt to taste
  • optimal ingredients:
    • Chicken
    • Carrot
    • Green peas

↑

Description

  1. Wash the rice thorougly and set it aside
  2. Blend the tomatoes, red bell peppers, and onion together.
    • Blend until smooth,
    • Do not add to much water
  3. Heat the vegetable oil in alarge pot.
  4. Add the tomato paste and fry for a few minutes.

    5. Be careful not to burn the tomatopaste

  5. Add the blended tomato paste miture and cook for about 10-15 minutes.
  6. Add curry powder, thyme, and salt.
  7. Add the chicken stock and bring the miture to a boil.
  8. Ad the washed rice and strir properly.
  9. Cover the pot and cook on low heat until the rice becomes soft.
    • Check the rice occasionally.
    • Add a litle water if necessary.
  10. Serve hot with your preferred side dish.

↑

Description

  • Tips: Cook the rice on low heat to prevent burning.
  • Notes: You can add chicken, beef, or fish .
  • tips: Allow the rice to rest for a few minutes before serving.
